# Content Readability & Scannability

## 1. Paragraph Rhythm
- Keep paragraphs compact: 2 to 4 sentences maximum.
- Avoid large monolithic blocks of prose that discourage scanning on mobile devices.
- Vary sentence length to maintain reading momentum and natural cadence.

## 2. Formatting Elements
- **Bullet & Numbered Lists:** Use lists when breaking down feature criteria, step-by-step implementation sequences, or comparison criteria.
- **Comparison Tables:** Synthesize build vs. buy decisions, off-the-shelf vs. custom trade-offs, and tool specifications in clean tables.
- **Callout Cards & Quotations:** Highlight critical architectural warnings, security considerations, or operational rules.

## 3. Cognitive Accessibility
- Frame concepts around real business problems before detailing code or database schemas.
- Ensure all technical terms have intuitive explanations for non-technical stakeholders (e.g., explaining why webhooks prevent polling bottlenecks).
