# Blog Writing Guidelines & Voice

## 1. Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Authoritative, measured, pragmatic, transparent, and educational.
- **Perspective:** Experienced systems architects who have built and maintained production code.
- **Language:** Clear technical English. Avoid jargon used for its own sake; define complex terms in context.
- **Philosophy Alignment:** Embody "Research. Build. Automate. Scale." — demonstrate the thought process before prescribing a technical solution.

## 2. Article Structure Rules
1. **Title:** Compelling, 50–65 characters, containing the primary keyword naturally.
2. **Introduction (150–200 words):** Hook the reader immediately with an operational bottleneck, establish why conventional workarounds break, and introduce the strategic solution. Do not label this section "Introduction".
3. **Core Sections (H2 & H3):**
   - Single H1 per page.
   - Distinct, logically ordered H2 sections covering core dimensions of the problem.
   - H3 tags for nested breakdowns, workflows, or sub-comparisons.
   - Integrate primary keyword naturally in 1–2 subheadings.
4. **Practical Examples:** Real-world scenarios, code snippets, architectural diagrams, or verified case study context from Achaia Labs' portfolio.
5. **External Resources (2–4 links):** Official developer documentation (Shopify docs, Google Search Central, PostgreSQL, etc.), standards bodies, or recognized industry benchmarks.
6. **Internal Links:** Contextual links to Achaia Labs service pages (`/services/erp`, `/services/shopify`, `/services/apps`, `/services/ai-automation`), case studies (`/work/...`), and related blog posts.
7. **Service Integration:** Position Achaia Labs naturally as an engineering partner without aggressive sales copy.
8. **Conclusion (100–150 words):** Summarize actionable next steps and strategic priorities.
9. **FAQ Section (4–6 questions):** Direct, concise answers addressing actual customer objections and search queries.
