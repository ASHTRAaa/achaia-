# Content Quality & Pre-Publication Checklist

Before publishing any article, verify every item:

### 1. Intent & Editorial Integrity
- [ ] Does the article directly satisfy the primary search intent without fluff?
- [ ] Is the primary keyword present naturally in the title, URL, intro, and 1-2 headings?
- [ ] Is the word count within the 1,000–1,500 word range?
- [ ] Are all paragraphs concise (2-4 sentences)?
- [ ] Is the tone professional, authoritative, and educational?
- [ ] Does it avoid repetitive AI filler, cliches ("In today's fast-paced digital world"), and hyperbole?

### 2. Technical & Citation Accuracy
- [ ] Are all external links pointed to authoritative, verified URLs (e.g. `shopify.dev`, `developers.google.com`)?
- [ ] Do external links open in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`?
- [ ] Are all internal links verified against existing site routes (`/services/...`, `/work/...`)?
- [ ] Are all case study references historically accurate based on actual Achaia Labs deliverables?

### 3. SEO & Conversion Requirements
- [ ] Exactly one H1 tag on the page.
- [ ] Correct heading hierarchy (no skipping from H2 to H4).
- [ ] Unique meta title (50-65 chars) and meta description (140-160 chars).
- [ ] Valid Schema.org JSON-LD (BlogPosting & BreadcrumbList).
- [ ] Contextual CTA embedded with connection to the SendMyForm API.
- [ ] 4-6 high-value FAQs with concise answers.
