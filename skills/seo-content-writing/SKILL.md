# SEO Content Writing Skill

## Overview
This skill governs the voice, structure, substance, and editorial quality of all articles published on the Achaia Labs platform.

## Guiding Principles
- **People-First Engineering Perspective:** Write for founders, operations leads, and technical decision-makers who value clarity, engineering accuracy, and operational reality over hype.
- **Substantive Depth:** Target 1,000 to 1,500 words per article. Deliver tangible blueprints, system trade-offs, and practical execution details rather than generic superficial summaries.
- **No Fabricated Claims:** Never invent client metrics, inflated ROIs, or nonexistent company partnerships. Always cite verified case studies or identify educational examples clearly.
- **Editorial Structure:** Follow the 9-part standard layout: SEO title, compelling hook/introduction, logically sequenced H2/H3 sections with tables/lists, genuine examples, verified external links, contextual internal links, soft service positioning, actionable conclusion, and targeted FAQs.
