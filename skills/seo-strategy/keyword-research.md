# Keyword Research Methodology

## 1. Objective
Identify high-intent, commercially valuable search queries that match Achaia Labs' technical service capabilities without relying on vanity keywords.

## 2. Selection Criteria
- **Commercial Viability:** Does the query indicate a business owner, operations manager, CTO, or founder seeking software engineering, automation, or custom CRM solutions?
- **Intent Clarity:** Is the query unambiguous, allowing a single well-structured article to answer the user's explicit question and implicit decision criteria?
- **Entity Richness:** Does the topic connect naturally to technical concepts (e.g., GraphQL API, webhooks, PostgreSQL schema, ATS vs. CRM, liquid templating)?

## 3. Keyword Clustering
Group secondary queries and semantic long-tail variations under the primary keyword:
- **Primary Keyword:** Central target with the highest relevant search demand.
- **Secondary Keywords:** Specific variations, sub-questions, and synonyms addressed in dedicated H2/H3 sections.
- **LSI & Entities:** Industry vocabulary, technical terms, and related operational systems.

## 4. Avoiding Hallucinated Metrics
Never fabricate search volume, keyword difficulty, or cost-per-click values. When third-party tool data is unavailable, prioritize qualitative evaluation: buyer intent, sales relevance, and organic competitiveness on current Google SERPs.
