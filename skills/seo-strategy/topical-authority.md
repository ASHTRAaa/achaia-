# Topical Authority & Semantic Graph Building

## 1. Pillar-Cluster Architecture
Topical authority is established when search engines recognize a site as a comprehensive, reliable source across an entire operational discipline.

For Achaia Labs, three core topical pillars govern the content engine:
1. **Custom Business Systems & CRM Engineering:**
   - Real estate CRM workflows
   - Recruitment agency databases & ATS integrations
   - Marketing agency CRM evaluation & multi-client architectures
   - Agency selection criteria (CRM agency guide)
2. **E-Commerce & Shopify Engineering:**
   - Shopify developer partner evaluation
   - Shopify API & headless architecture (Storefront/Admin GraphQL)
3. **Product Engineering & Digital Automation:**
   - Small business web development & partner selection
   - Small business website planning & launch frameworks
   - Practical AI automation agency workflows & reliability engineering

## 2. Cross-Linking & Semantic Proximity
- Articles within a pillar cross-reference each other to strengthen topical graphs.
- Informational guides link contextually to commercial partner guides.
- Service landing pages receive relevant contextual inbound links from high-authority educational content.
