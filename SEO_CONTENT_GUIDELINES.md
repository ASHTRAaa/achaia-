# SEO Content Guidelines & Editorial Framework — Achaia Labs

## 1. Editorial Voice & Philosophy
Achaia Labs operates under the philosophy **"Research. Build. Automate. Scale."** Our blog content must mirror the quality and clarity of high-grade engineering documentation:

- **Target Audience:** Founders, CTOs, operations leads, and technical decision-makers.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, clear, pragmatic, and educational. Avoid fluffy marketing speak, buzzwords, and vague promises.
- **Word Count:** 1,000 to 1,500 words per article. Depth and utility take precedence over superficial filler.
- **People-First Integrity:** Focus on solving real architectural, operational, and commercial problems before mentioning agency capabilities.

---

## 2. Standard 9-Part Article Structure

Every article must adhere to this exact sequence:

### Part 1: SEO Title
- Naturally includes the primary keyword.
- Length: 50–65 characters.
- Compelling, descriptive, and free of keyword stuffing.

### Part 2: Introduction (150–200 words)
- Directly addresses the core business or operational problem.
- Explains why typical workarounds fail or produce technical debt.
- Introduces the practical framework explored in the guide.
- **Constraint:** Do not use the literal heading "Introduction".

### Part 3: Main Content (H2 & H3 Hierarchy)
- **Exactly one `<h1>` per page.**
- **`<h2>` tags** for major thematic sections and frameworks.
- **`<h3>` tags** for sub-points, steps, technical specifications, and feature lists.
- Keep paragraphs to 2–4 sentences for effortless mobile reading.
- Include structured comparison tables, bullet points, and numbered operational sequences.
- Naturally include the primary keyword in 1–2 relevant subheadings.

### Part 4: Practical Examples & Case Evidence
- Ground recommendations in real business scenarios.
- Reference verified Achaia Labs portfolio achievements (`/work/...`) where relevant:
  - *Shopify:* Moms & Moon (`/work/moms-and-moon`), Mandala Good Vibes (`/work/mandala-good-vibes`).
  - *Custom ERP & Governance:* SKMT Trust (`/work/skmt-trust`).
  - *Digital Catalog & Inquiries:* Gaurav Synthetics (`/work/gaurav-synthetics`), Divine Enterprises (`/work/divine-enterprises`).
  - *AI & App Workflows:* Helios AI Platform (`/work/helios`), Fox Wing Media (`/work/fox-wing-media`).
  - *In-House Developer Products:* SendMyForm (`/products/sendmyform`).
- For hypothetical scenarios, clearly state them as educational examples.

### Part 5: Relevant External Resources (2–4 Outbound Links)
- Cite authoritative developer documentation, official platform standards, or reputable research.
- Always verify URLs (`https://shopify.dev/docs`, `https://developers.google.com/search/docs`, `https://www.postgresql.org/docs/`, etc.).
- Use descriptive anchor text; open in new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.

### Part 6: Internal Links
- Link to the corresponding Achaia Labs service vertical (`/services/erp`, `/services/shopify`, `/services/apps`, `/services/ai-automation`).
- Link to relevant case studies (`/work/...`).
- Link to related blog articles within the same topical cluster.

### Part 7: Natural Product & Service Integration
- Present Achaia Labs as a capable engineering partner where custom development or automation is contextually necessary.
- Maintain an educational focus—the article must deliver standalone value even if the reader does not contact us.

### Part 8: Conclusion (100–150 words)
- Summarize critical strategic takeaways.
- Provide a clear, actionable path forward for the business.

### Part 9: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ Section)
- 4 to 6 genuinely useful, common questions reflecting user search queries.
- Answers must be concise, accurate, and independently understandable.

---

## 3. Formatting & Readability Rules
- **No Monolithic Paragraphs:** Maximum 4 sentences per paragraph.
- **Lists and Tables:** Mandatory where feature comparisons, decision criteria, or implementation steps are detailed.
- **Code & Payloads:** Use clear JSON, GraphQL, or pseudocode formatting where technical APIs or schemas are discussed.
- **Zero Hallucinated Metrics:** Never invent client ROI figures, conversion percentages, or fake testimonials.
